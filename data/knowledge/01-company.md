# Fortivex AI — Company Overview

## Who we are

Fortivex AI is a Bengaluru-based AI product company founded in 2023. We build autonomous voice agents that handle inbound and outbound sales conversations for B2B teams. Our mission is to give every sales team enterprise-grade coverage without adding headcount.

## Who we serve

Our ideal customer profile is a B2B company with 10–500 sales representatives and a high volume of inbound or outbound calls. We work especially well with:

- B2B SaaS and technology companies
- Fintech, insurance and financial services
- Healthcare and diagnostics providers
- E-commerce and D2C brands with large support/sales call volumes
- Real estate and education enrolment teams

## The problem we solve

Sales teams lose revenue because:

- Inbound leads wait hours for a first response.
- SDRs spend most of their day on repetitive qualification calls.
- Call notes are inconsistent, so CRM data is unreliable.
- Coverage stops at 6 pm and on weekends.
- Multilingual markets are expensive to staff.

## Our value proposition

- **24/7 coverage:** every lead gets an immediate, natural conversation.
- **Consistent qualification:** the same discovery framework on every call.
- **Clean CRM data:** structured lead fields, scores and summaries after every call.
- **Lower cost per qualified meeting:** automate the top of the funnel and route hot leads to humans.
- **Multilingual reach:** English, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, German and Portuguese.
- **Fast time to value:** a working pilot in 2–4 weeks.

## Illustrative pilot benchmarks

These are illustrative averages from controlled pilots, not audited customer results:

- 38% increase in qualified meetings booked
- 62% lower cost per qualified lead
- 4.6/5 average caller satisfaction
- 70% of routine qualification handled without a human

## How we are different

- Voice-first, not a chatbot bolted onto a phone line.
- Local-first and private deployment options for regulated buyers.
- Transparent, explainable lead scoring.
- CRM and calendar integrations included, not sold as custom projects.
- Human handoff and guardrails designed for enterprise sales teams.
