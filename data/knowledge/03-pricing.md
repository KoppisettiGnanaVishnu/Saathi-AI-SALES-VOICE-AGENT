# Pricing and Plans

## Plans

### Starter — $199 per month
- 300 call minutes per month
- 1 phone number
- English voice agent
- Email support
- Basic CRM export

### Growth — $499 per month
- 1,000 call minutes per month
- 3 phone numbers
- Multilingual agents (English + Hindi + one additional language)
- CRM and calendar integrations
- Lead scoring, sentiment analysis and call summaries
- Priority email and chat support

### Enterprise — custom pricing
- Volume-based minutes
- Unlimited numbers and languages
- SSO/SAML, audit logs and role-based access
- Private VPC or on-premise deployment
- Custom CRM, telephony and data-warehouse integrations
- Dedicated solution architect and SLA

## Overage

- Starter: $0.35 per additional minute
- Growth: $0.29 per additional minute
- Enterprise: committed-volume rate agreed in the contract

## Setup and onboarding

- Starter and Growth: no setup fee; guided self-serve onboarding.
- Enterprise: onboarding and custom integration work quoted separately.
- Standard onboarding takes 2–4 weeks, including knowledge-base ingestion, voice tuning, CRM mapping and a pilot.

## Discounts

- 15% discount on annual prepayment.
- Startup and nonprofit discounts are available on request.
- Pilot offer: a 14-day paid pilot with 100 minutes so teams can validate quality before committing.

## What is included

All plans include the knowledge-base RAG pipeline, call recording controls, consent configuration, lead scoring, call summaries and standard security controls.

## Commercial model

Pricing is per workspace, not per seat. This makes it economical to give every rep and every campaign access to the agent. Enterprise contracts can be structured per minute, per qualified meeting or per resolution, depending on the buyer's preference.
