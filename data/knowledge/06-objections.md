# Objection Handling Guide

## "It's too expensive."

Acknowledge the concern, then reframe against the cost of the status quo. A single SDR costs far more than a Growth plan, and the agent handles the repetitive top of the funnel so humans focus on closing. Offer the 14-day pilot to prove cost per qualified meeting before a larger commitment.

## "We already have an IVR or phone tree."

An IVR routes callers through menus; it does not understand intent or hold a conversation. Fortivex AI qualifies the caller, answers product questions, books meetings and updates the CRM. It can sit in front of the existing IVR or replace the sales line entirely.

## "AI sounds robotic."

Modern neural voices are natural, support barge-in and can be tuned to the brand's tone. The agent keeps replies short and conversational. Offer a live sample call and a voice-selection session during onboarding.

## "Our customers won't like talking to AI."

The agent discloses that it is AI, handles routine questions quickly, and transfers to a human on request. In pilots, most callers value immediate answers over waiting on hold. Consent and disclosure are configurable by region.

## "Security and compliance are a blocker."

Fortivex AI supports TLS 1.3, AES-256 at rest, RBAC, SSO, audit logs, PII redaction, configurable retention and regional data residency. SOC 2 Type II is in progress, GDPR/DPDP support is available, and HIPAA is supported with a BAA on Enterprise. We can complete the buyer's security review and sign an NDA/DPA.

## "We'll build this in-house."

Building the voice loop is the easy part. Production quality requires telephony, low-latency streaming, multilingual voices, RAG evaluation, CRM sync, guardrails, compliance and ongoing model maintenance. Fortivex AI delivers a working pilot in 2–4 weeks and keeps improving it. Buy versus build usually favors buying for teams that want to focus on their core product.

## "We tried a chatbot and it didn't work."

Chatbots fail when they are ungrounded and cannot take action. This agent is voice-first, answers from an approved knowledge base, books real slots and writes to the CRM. It is measured on qualified meetings and CRM completeness, not message volume.

## "Integration will be a nightmare."

Prebuilt connectors cover the major CRMs, calendars and telephony providers. Anything else goes through a documented REST API and webhooks. A typical integration is completed during the 2–4 week onboarding.

## "We need to think about it."

Agree, and propose a low-risk next step: a 30-minute demo using the buyer's own use case and documents, followed by a 14-day pilot. Ask what specifically needs to be true for them to move forward.
