# Illustrative Case Studies

> These are illustrative pilot scenarios used for demo purposes. They are not audited customer results.

## B2B SaaS — inbound demo requests

A 40-rep SaaS company received 600 inbound demo requests per month. First response averaged 6 hours and 38% of leads were never contacted. An AI voice agent handled immediate qualification, answered pricing and security questions, and booked qualified demos.

Illustrative outcome: first response under 60 seconds, 31% more demos booked, and 22 hours of SDR time saved per week.

## Fintech — outbound reactivation

A lending platform had 12,000 dormant applicants. The team used an outbound voice agent to re-qualify interest, confirm eligibility and route warm leads to loan officers.

Illustrative outcome: 18% reactivation rate, 2.4x more qualified callbacks, and a 47% lower cost per reactivated lead.

## Healthcare — appointment reminders and rescheduling

A diagnostics chain received high call volumes for appointment changes. The agent handled rescheduling, confirmed preparation instructions and escalated clinical questions to staff.

Illustrative outcome: 64% of reschedules handled without a human, 29% reduction in no-shows, and improved staff focus on clinical work.

## Real estate — lead qualification

A property developer received thousands of campaign leads across three languages. The agent qualified budget, location and timeline, then booked site visits.

Illustrative outcome: 3.1x more site visits booked and 55% of routine qualification automated.

## Why these scenarios matter

They show the same core workflow across industries: answer instantly, qualify consistently, take action in the CRM, and hand off the right conversations to humans.
